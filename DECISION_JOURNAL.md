# Watchtower Decision Journal

The one question after every real conversation with Watchtower:

> **Did this conversation increase or decrease my trust in Watchtower as a thinking partner?**

Trust is the target. Reasoning, research, debate, and recommendation quality are
used **only to diagnose why trust moved** — never optimized for on their own.

This journal is the evolving evaluation dataset. One entry per real interaction.
Over time it tells us whether Watchtower is actually becoming a better co-founder.

## How to use

After each `watchtower chat` session, add an entry at the top of **Entries** using
the template. Keep it short and honest. Fill **Trust** first; the rest explains it.

## Template

```
### YYYY-MM-DD — <short problem title>

- Problem: <what you brought to Watchtower>
- Recommendation: <what it recommended> (confidence it reported: <n%>)
- Founder response: <your pushback / how the debate went>
- Final decision: <what you actually decided>
- Trust: increased | decreased | unchanged
- Why (diagnostic): <which axis drove it — reasoning / research / debate / recommendation — one line>
```
